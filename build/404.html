Good questions

A few things
you might wonder.

What does Revera do with a recording?+

Revera saves recordings and transcripts in the app on your device. Depending on the feature you use, audio or transcript text is sent through Revera’s service to OpenAI for transcription, summaries, translation, or transcript questions. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I record without an internet connection?+

Yes. You can record offline. Online transcription and AI processing need a connection; Revera can queue recordings and process them when you’re back online.

Can I search and revisit a particular moment?+

Search across transcripts to find a word or phrase, then use timestamped transcript segments to jump back to a moment in the recording.

Which languages does Revera support?+

Revera supports transcription in 14+ languages, including Arabic, Chinese, English, French, German, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, and Turkish. Language availability can depend on the feature and provider.

How do subscriptions and payments work?+

Revera is free to download and offers in-app purchases. Subscription availability, pricing, and billing terms are shown by Apple in the App Store and in the app before purchase.
